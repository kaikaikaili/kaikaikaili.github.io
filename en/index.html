About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the School of Atmospheric Sciences, Nanjing University (B.S. through the Elite Undergraduate Program). My research interests lie at the intersection of atmospheric science, radar meteorology, and deep learning: using advanced AI algorithms to untangle complex cloud physical processes and the vertical structure of precipitation, with a particular focus on quantitative retrieval problems in extreme weather systems such as typhoons.

Research

Ongoing Project · Student Basic Research Program

Deep-Learning-Driven Quantitative Retrieval of Vertical Velocity of Typhoon Precipitation from Spaceborne Dual-Frequency Radar
Based on GPM-DPR dual-frequency radar observations, exploring deep learning approaches for retrieving the vertical velocity of precipitation particles.
